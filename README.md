## NestMate — Jira Project Planning Case Study
### 1. Project Overview
   NestMate is a PG and hostel finder platform. This case study demonstrates how I applied Agile/Scrum methodology to organize its development workflow in Jira by creating epics, user stories, and three sprints, estimating story points, and tracking task completion. The Jira setup was created retrospectively to showcase my project planning and tracking skills.

### 2. My Role
   Planned and organized the project workflow in Jira by creating epics and user stories, structuring three sprints, assigning story points, and tracking work-item statuses. Documented the project structure and explored Jira reporting features to understand progress tracking and sprint management. This was a retrospective exercise to demonstrate my project management skills.

### 3. Key Skills Demonstrated
- Epic and User Story creation in Jira
- Sprint planning and timeline management  
- Story point estimation
- Work item tracking and status management
- Agile/Scrum methodology application
- Jira reporting and velocity tracking
